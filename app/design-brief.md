# IntelliHorizon design brief

Design read: Grade 6 to 10 students in Bangladesh, often on a low-cost Android phone, who deserve a learning space that feels like wonder and treats them as thinkers, never as scores.

Concept spine: "The Understanding Sky". Every concept is a star sewn into a night sky with kantha running stitches. A star only brightens when the learner shows evidence of understanding, and it reaches full light only when the idea survives being put in disguise (new context, new representation, the other language, a reversed argument). Kantha is the stitch-by-stitch craft of Bengal: the sky is literally stitched together by the learner's own reasoning.

Delivery tier: editorial (user chose a non-animated site). Motion is micro and motivated: stitch-draw and star-ignite on evidence, nothing passive.

Animation mode: non-animated (user picked Non-animated at intake)

Locked palette (one theme per page; universe pages are night, lesson pages are daylight paper):
- Night sky: #121B3E (deepest), #1A2550, #243163, stitch line #3A4880
- Kantha cloth text: #F3EFE6 high, #BFC3DA mid, #8E95B8 low
- Daylight paper: #EEF0F8 page, #FFFFFF sheet, #18224B ink, #4A5480 ink-mid
- Accent (the lit thread): #F6C453. One UI accent only. Used for primary actions, lit stars and stitches.
- Subject threads (identity only, never on buttons): math #E2A93B, physics #E8644A, chemistry #4FB5A5, biology #8DBF5A, english #7FA7EA, bangla #E57AA0, ict #A9B6D8, social #C98B62
- Feedback: right #3E9E6C, needs-another-look #D0573F (never red shaming copy)
Defense: indigo night plus marigold thread comes from kantha quilts and Bangladeshi monsoon nights; it is not near-black and the accent is a warm yellow thread, avoiding the banned families.

Locked type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, Latin) + Hind Siliguri (body and all Bangla, designed for Bangla UI legibility with matching Latin). Bangla text is never set in the display face.

Corner language: containers 14px soft; small chips are full-round "thread beads" (written rule).

Section plan (home): Sky canvas (interactive constellations) / next useful step panel / worlds strip. Lessons: Wonder (predict) / Explore (simulation) / Make sense (adaptive repair) / Teach Rumi / Shapeshifter Trials / Then and now.

Asset plan: account had 0 credits at build time, so no generated imagery yet. All visuals are real interactive components (sky, Drop Lab simulation, roti fractions, evidence board). Cover and favicon image to be generated when credits are added; a hand-built SVG monogram is the interim favicon.

CTA inventory: IgniteButton (primary, stitched border that fills with thread on press), StitchLink (secondary text link with running-stitch underline), BeadToggle (language and option chips), ConfidenceDial (three-bead confidence selector), ChallengeLink ("That's not what I was thinking"), ReadAloudBead.

Honesty contract: the prototype's adaptive engine is a transparent evidence model with authored misconception maps. It never claims to read minds; every inference shows its evidence and can be challenged.
